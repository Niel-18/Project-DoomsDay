THE STORY SO FAR

AI can write code in seconds. But can it be trusted? On 12 December 2026, the Codeoholics Community brings you a day built to go beyond a regular workshop: an expert-led session, then three missions that put your skills, your judgment and your creativity to the test.

Explore modern AI tools, prompt engineering, AI-assisted coding and more. Then prove that human logic and critical thinking still matter when machines do the heavy lifting.

  • Prompt Engineering
  • AI-Assisted Coding
  • Debugging
  • Game Prototyping
  • Startup Pitching
  • Responsible AI
